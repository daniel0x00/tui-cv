Agentic SOC.

Security operations, with people in control.

At SKF, I built an identity-first platform and internal Cyber MCP to connect specialised agents, documented procedures and security tools. The aim: give analysts useful evidence and reduce repetitive work, while keeping consequential decisions with people.

02

Reasoning and execution have separate routes.

The model gateway routes requests for reasoning. A separate Function gateway governs tool execution. Evidence comes back to analysts, who review the findings and any consequential action.

A model can recommend an action; it cannot grant itself access.

03

Where the platform is used.

  • 01

    Identity & estate

    Bring together identities, privileged roles, device ownership and Azure inventory to examine access and coverage.

  • 02

    Vulnerability & exposure

    Investigate endpoint vulnerabilities, remediation ageing and external exposure using reconnaissance, DNS and reputation checks.

  • 03

    Detect & investigate

    Add Defender hunting, identity context and threat intelligence to Sentinel alerts for analyst triage.

  • 04

    ITSM & knowledge

    Link ServiceNow incidents and ownership with SharePoint playbooks and markdown-first procedures.

  • 05

    Reporting & assurance

    Produce private posture reports and support TISAX guidance and audit-evidence lookup.

  • 06

    Human-led response

    Present findings and proposed next steps to analysts. Active containment automation remains pending.

An investigation, step by step.

Illustrative example · the analyst remains responsible for the decision

  1. 01

    Receive

    A Sentinel alert provides the entities and the question to investigate.

  2. 02

    Investigate

    Agents follow documented procedures and request identity-gated, audited queries.

  3. 03

    Enrich

    Device posture, identity context and threat intelligence add context.

  4. 04

    Document

    The incident record brings together evidence, uncertainty and suggested next steps.

  5. 05

    Review

    An analyst reviews the findings and approves the response. The example does not imply automatic containment.

05

Boundaries built into the workflow.

  • 01

    Identity

    Entra SSO, app roles and managed identities govern access without shared operator passwords.

  • 02

    Audit before execute

    Audited routes record the request server-side before execution and stop if recording fails.

  • 03

    Least privilege

    Scoped permissions and network-isolated connectors limit what tools can reach.

  • 04

    Human approval & PII

    Approval gates, prompt guardrails and personal-data controls support accountable use.
