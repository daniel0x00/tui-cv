03

Six capabilities. One operating model.

  • 01

    Identity & estate

    Connect identities, privileged roles, device ownership and Azure inventory to understand access, coverage and risk.

  • 02

    Vulnerability & exposure

    Track endpoint vulnerabilities, remediation ageing and external exposure; use reconnaissance, DNS and reputation checks to investigate.

  • 03

    Detect & investigate

    Enrich Sentinel alerts with Defender hunting, identity context and threat intelligence for evidence-led triage.

  • 04

    ITSM & knowledge

    Connect ServiceNow incidents and ownership with SharePoint playbooks and markdown-first procedures, keeping investigation knowledge reusable.

  • 05

    Reporting & assurance

    Turn telemetry into private reports and posture summaries; support TISAX control guidance and audit-evidence lookup.

  • 06

    Human-led response

    Give analysts documented findings and proposed next steps. Active containment automation remains pending, not a live capability.

04

From alert to analyst.

Illustrative workflow · from evidence to a human decision.

  1. 01

    Receive

    A Sentinel alert supplies the starting entities and investigation question.

  2. 02

    Investigate

    Agents follow procedures and request identity-gated, audited queries.

  3. 03

    Enrich

    Device posture, identity context and threat intelligence inform the assessment.

  4. 04

    Document

    Evidence, uncertainty and suggested next steps accompany the incident.

  5. 05

    Review

    An analyst checks the findings and approves the response; no automatic containment is implied.

05

Control is part of the design.

  • 01

    Identity

    Entra SSO, app roles and managed identities govern access, rather than shared operator passwords.

  • 02

    Audit before execute

    Audited routes write server-side records before execution and abort if recording fails.

  • 03

    Least privilege

    Scoped permissions and network-isolated connectors constrain what tools can reach.

  • 04

    Human approval & PII

    Approval gates, prompt guardrails and personal-data controls keep automation accountable to people.
